Lincmox Light up your LincStation

Take full control of the status LEDs and the front LED strip of the LincPlus LincStation N1 when the machine runs Proxmox VE.

  • Full control of every status LED: power, SATA, NVMe and network
  • Front LED strip: pick colors and brightness like on the real device
  • Automatic blink: monitor network traffic and light up on activity
  • Three surfaces: embedded Web UI, CLI and REST API
